StatMate™ Descriptive statistic

Descriptive statistic conducts column-wise statistical analysis and computes skewness and kurtosis to classify distributions. It evaluates probabilistic models like Gaussian, lognormal and gamma, ranks them by log-likelihood and generates interactive HTML results.
